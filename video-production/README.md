# Template 02 — Matching Cinematic Wedding Video

## Goal
Create a vertical 9:16 cinematic invitation video matching the Royal Wedding Website.

## Visual identity
- Deep burgundy / wine velvet
- Antique-gold filigree and borders
- Ivory typography
- Elegant roses and restrained floating petals
- Premium Indian royal palace mood
- No SA monogram/logo; branding is omitted unless explicitly requested

## Suggested 20–25 second storyboard
1. **0–3s — Royal opening:** burgundy velvet and antique-gold border emerge from darkness; slow golden light sweep. Soft instrumental music only; no voiceover.
2. **3–6s — Invitation reveal:** ornate ivory invitation panel appears with delicate gold flourishes.
3. **6–10s — Couple names:** reveal “Riya & Aarav” in elegant serif typography with subtle gold shimmer.
4. **10–14s — Date:** show “12 December 2026” with a slow cinematic push-in.
5. **14–19s — Event details:** show ceremony time and venue clearly; preserve comfortable text margins.
6. **19–23s — Closing frame:** “We await your presence” and a restrained floral ornament.

## Production rules
- Output: 1080 × 1920, 9:16.
- Keep important text within social-platform safe margins.
- Use gentle transitions, no rapid flashing, no distracting particles behind text.
- Music only by default; do not generate narration/voiceover unless requested.
- Confirm every name, date, time, venue and spelling before rendering.
- Export MP4 and review on a phone before delivery.

This guide contains a storyboard and production instructions, not a rendered MP4. Generate and quality-check the actual video separately.
