# Video production brief — Template 11

Project: template11-Blue Aesthetic The Wedding Invitation Flyer
Format: 9:16 vertical, 20–30 seconds
Visual style: midnight/navy blue, ivory and restrained antique gold; elegant Indian wedding atmosphere.
Audio: no voiceover, no spoken dialogue; instrumental music only if available.
Use editable placeholders until customer details are provided.

1. Invitation reveal: ornate blue cover with delicate gold accents, slow reveal, soft golden particles.
2. Couple names: clean centered names with elegant typography and gentle camera motion.
3. Wedding details: date, time and venue displayed clearly; avoid text warping.
4. Final celebration: blue-lit floral mandap, warm gold lights and a graceful closing shot.

Maintain the same palette and ornament style across all four scenes. No unrelated themes or extra scenes.
