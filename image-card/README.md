# Template 03 — Matching Wedding Invitation Image Card

## Goal
Create a high-resolution, WhatsApp-ready invitation card matching the website and cinematic video.

## Design direction
- Deep burgundy velvet background
- Symmetrical antique-gold ornamental frame
- Large ivory parchment panel with generous clear space
- Delicate floral accents only at corners
- Elegant serif couple names; clean readable event details
- No SA monogram/logo
- Do not place busy ornaments directly behind important text

## Suggested content order
1. Together with their families
2. Riya & Aarav
3. Wedding date
4. Ceremony time
5. Venue and city
6. We await your presence

## Export and QA
- Create a high-resolution portrait version suitable for WhatsApp sharing.
- Keep a 9:16 variant if the customer also needs a Story/Reel cover.
- Check all names, date, time, venue, spelling, contrast and margins.
- Keep text editable where possible; proofread AI-rendered text carefully.
- Replace all fictional sample details before customer delivery.

This folder documents the reusable design direction. The final PNG/JPG artwork must be created and reviewed separately.
